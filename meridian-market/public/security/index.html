How we keep your account and payments safe

Here is exactly what the store does with your password, your session and your card details.

🔑

Passwords are hashed

We store a bcrypt hash with a unique salt, never the password itself. Not even the team can read it.

🍪

Sessions stay on the server

Your login lives in our database. The browser only keeps an httpOnly cookie that page scripts can't read, and it expires after 7 days.

💳

No stored card numbers

Card numbers are checked at checkout and then discarded. Only the last four digits are saved with your order.

🛡️

Safe database queries

Every query uses parameters, so text you type can't change the SQL. Account pages refuse requests from anyone who isn't logged in.

🔁

Fresh session on login

We issue a brand-new session ID each time you log in, which blocks session-fixation attacks.

🔒

HTTPS in production

When deployed, the site should run over TLS so passwords and cookies are encrypted in transit.

Keep your account safe

A few habits that protect you on any site.

  • Use a password you don't use anywhere else, at least 8 characters with letters and numbers.
  • Log out when you shop on a shared or public computer.
  • We will never email you asking for your password or card number.
  • Change your password from Your account if you think someone else knows it.
