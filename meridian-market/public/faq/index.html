Orders

How do I place an order?
Add items to your cart with the + button, open the cart and choose “Go to checkout”. You'll enter a shipping address, pick a delivery speed, add a card, and review everything before placing the order.
Can I check out without an account?
You can fill a cart as a guest, but you need an account to pay. When you log in or sign up, everything in your guest cart moves into your account automatically.
Where can I see my past orders?
Go to Your account and open the Orders tab. Click any order to see its items, totals and shipping address.
Can I change an order after placing it?
Orders are confirmed right away, so we can't edit them. If something is wrong, place a new order and return the old one within 30 days.

Shipping & returns

How much is shipping?
Standard shipping is $5.99, or free when your subtotal is $50 or more. Express costs $14.99. Pickup from our San Francisco counter is always free.
How long does delivery take?
Standard arrives in 3–5 business days and express in 1–2. Pickup orders are ready the next day.
What is your return policy?
You can return any unused item within 30 days for a full refund. Refunds go back to the original card within 5–7 business days of us receiving the item.

Payment

Which cards do you accept?
Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover. Because this is a student project, no real charge is made; use a test number like 4242 4242 4242 4242.
Do you store my card number?
No. We only keep the last four digits so you can recognise the card in your order history. Read more about security.
How do promo codes work?
Enter the code in the order summary at checkout and press Apply. WELCOME15 gives 15% off and MERIDIAN10 gives 10% off. One code per order.

Your account

How do I reset my password?
If you're logged in, go to Your account, then the Password tab. You'll need your current password to set a new one.
How do I update my address?
Open the Profile tab in Your account. The address you save there is filled in for you at checkout.
How do I switch to dark mode?
Go to Settings and choose a theme under Appearance. Your choice is saved in this browser.
