A small store for the things you use every day.

Meridian started as a class project with a simple question: what would an online shop look like if it only sold things the team would actually buy for themselves?

Fewer, better things

Every product earns its spot. We would rather carry 24 good things than 2,400 average ones.

Honest prices

No fake "was" prices. When something is on sale, it really used to cost more.

Clear policies

Free shipping over $50, 30-day returns, and support that answers in plain words.

How we got here

The project grew over four course milestones.

  1. Milestone 1

    Wireframes for eleven pages and a product catalogue plan.

  2. Milestone 2

    Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript: the storefront, product filters and a browser-based cart.

  3. Milestone 3

    An Express server and PostgreSQL database, with real accounts, sessions and a saved cart.

  4. Milestone 4

    Checkout, order history, reviews and a wishlist, all backed by the database.
